Hope you're well! I just read the new Frontiers paper (Heine et al. 2025) and got really excited about the CLSM z-stacks and FISH data from the HOBIC experiments.

We're working on a spatial extension of the gLV model — a reaction-diffusion PDE in up to 3D (z = depth, x = flow direction, y = lateral):

   ∂φ_i/∂t = D_i ∇²φ_i − u·∇φ_i + φ_i(b_i + Σ_j A_ij φ_j)

The A matrix is already estimated from the HOBIC time-series. To fit the diffusion coefficients D_i and validate the predicted spatial gradients we would ideally need:

   1. z-profiles (depth axis): species-resolved FISH intensity vs z at each time point — this is the minimum we need for the 1D model.
   2. Full 3D CLSM/FISH stacks (x, y, z): raw .lif or .czi files for the flow chamber time series (Day 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21) — these would let us go to a full 3D reaction-diffusion model and capture colony patterning along the flow axis as well.

Even just the z-collapsed profiles would already be hugely useful, and the full stacks would open up the 3D extension.

Would it be possible to get hold of either — raw stacks or processed profiles — directly or via Katharina? Happy to discuss co-authorship or acknowledgement.

\begin{frame}{Research objectives and overview}
\protect\hypertarget{research-objectives-and-overview}{}
We model the \textbf{dysbiosis of the oral-biofilm community} relevant
to peri-implantitis as the time evolution of community composition
(SIIRI consortium).

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Clinical problem: a microbial community that transitions from
  commensal to dysbiotic around titanium implants.
\item
  Research question: \textbf{what drives this transition} --- and what
  does metabolism, computed from genomes, tell us about ecological
  interactions?
\item
  Central thesis: \textbf{metabolism constrains the sign of ecological
  interactions.}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.4em}

This defense presents ecological ODE inference, a metabolic sign prior,
and a spatial extension as a coherent research arc.
\end{frame}

\protect\hypertarget{graphical-abstract}{}
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth,height=0.78\textheight]{results/figures/concept_overview_pub.png}

Metabolism fixes the \textbf{sign} of interactions; dysbiosis appears as

\begin{frame}{The research arc}
\protect\hypertarget{the-research-arc}{}
The work has two stages:

\begin{enumerate}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Original (first half)} --- a 5-species in-vitro system,
  inferred by \textbf{GPU Bayesian ODE inference}. TMCMC (\(10^4\)
  particles) recovered the posterior for four attractors \textbf{CS / CH
  / DS / DH} (commensal/dysbiotic × static/HOBIC).
\item
  \textbf{Extension (second half)} --- carry the framework to real data:
  a \textbf{metabolic AGORA sign prior} + \textbf{in-vivo longitudinal
  16S} + a \textbf{spatial PDE} + \textbf{FISH}.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.4em}

The first half establishes the dynamical-systems skeleton (attractors,
pH prediction); the second grounds it in real data via metabolic
constraints and spatial structure.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Core model (concise)}
\protect\hypertarget{core-model-concise}{}
Generalized Lotka--Volterra for absolute abundances \(x_i\):
\[\dot{x}_i = x_i\Big(b_i + \sum_{j=1}^{S} A_{ij}\,x_j\Big),\qquad S=10 .\]

Replicator / Hamilton form on the simplex \(\sum_i\varphi_i=1\) (16S is
compositional):
\[\dot{\varphi}_i = \varphi_i\Big[(A\varphi+b)_i - \varphi^{\!\top}(A\varphi+b)\Big].\]

\textbf{Sign prior:} from AGORA cross-feeding flux \(F_{ij}\) build
\[P_{ij}=\operatorname{sgn}(F_{ij})\in\{-1,0,+1\},\] constraining only
the \textbf{sign} of \(A\) (magnitude discarded by design).
\end{frame}

\textcolor{red}{This overstates it — the prior is sign-degenerate (honest test next).}

\begin{frame}{Honest validation}
\protect\hypertarget{honest-validation}{}
The naive 92\% is inflated by sign degeneracy. The correct control is a
permutation test:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Only the \textbf{cross-feeding direction} is independently validated:
  \[p=4\times10^{-4}\quad(z=+3.79,\ n_\pi=10^4).\]
\item
  The \textbf{competition direction is NOT validated} (chance level).
\item
  Across \textbf{two cohorts} (Dieckow × Botelho), prior-free,
  strong-pair signs agree at \textbf{89\%} (\(p\approx0.02\)).
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}

\textcolor{red}{The AGORA prior is not reproduced by the 16S dynamics — it is a
modelling choice, not a data-confirmed fact.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Network view}
\protect\hypertarget{network-view}{}
Structural analysis of the inferred \(A\) (class level):

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Veillonella (Negativicutes) is the metabolic sink} --- the
  receiver that aggregates lactate and other products.
\item
  The classic \textbf{Pg-keystone / Fn-bridge picture is NOT supported
  at class level.}
\item
  In dysbiosis \textbf{Pg centralises} (eigenvector centrality
  \(0.32\to0.51\)).
\item
  At the same time the \textbf{S. oralis--Veillonella mutualism breaks.}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.4em}

Dysbiosis is not a mere compositional change but a \textbf{rewiring} of
the interaction network.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Reaction--diffusion PDE}
\protect\hypertarget{reactiondiffusion-pde}{}
Extend the 0D gLV to depth-resolved reaction--diffusion along \(z\):
\[\partial_t\varphi_i = D_i\,\partial_z^2\varphi_i - u\,\partial_z\varphi_i + R_i(\varphi),\]
\(R_i\) the replicator reaction term (with \(A,b\) fixed from the
temporal fit); only \(D_i\) (diffusion) and \(u\) (advection) are
inferred from the depth profiles.

\vspace{0.4em}

\textcolor{red}{This is preliminary — the HPC parameter sweep is converging.}
The spatial fit grounds the model on an observable (FISH depth)
independent of the temporal dynamics.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusions}
\protect\hypertarget{conclusions}{}
\begin{enumerate}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Metabolism constrains the sign of ecological interactions}
  (\(P_{ij}=\operatorname{sgn}(F_{ij})\); magnitude is not used).
\item
  Dysbiosis is a \textbf{rewiring + spatial reorganisation}, with
  \emph{P. gingivalis} sinking deep (+30 µm).
\item
  That claim reproduces across \textbf{two cohorts} (Dieckow × Botelho,
  89\%, \(p\approx0.02\)) and an \textbf{independent mechanistic route}
  (COMETS dFBA).
\item
  The original 5-species ODE predicts pH independently (\(R^2=0.78\),
  LOO \(R^2=0.92\)).
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Outlook}
\protect\hypertarget{outlook}{}
\textbf{Near term}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Neutral-init LOO} (submitted) --- confirm \(p=4\times10^{-4}\)
  sign enrichment is data-driven, not warm-start artefact.
\item
  \textbf{Unified inference} --- TMCMC posteriors regularised by
  metabolic sign prior → credible intervals on in-vivo \(A_{ij}\).
\item
  \textbf{Asymmetric \(A\) channel} --- relax \(A_{ij}=A_{ji}\) for
  directional metabolite pairs; test whether competition direction
  recovers significance.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Medium term}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Fn knock-out prediction} --- omitting \emph{F. nucleatum}
  should collapse the dysbiotic \emph{Pg} surge (direct co-culture
  test).
\item
  \textbf{Full time-series PINN fit} --- turn placeholder \(D_i\) into
  measured diffusivities; couple \(O_2\)-PDE to Monod kinetics.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Long term}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Clinical dysbiosis index} --- 10-guild projection onto
  commensal--dysbiotic axis, calibrated from attractor states.
\item
  \textbf{Patient-specific MAGs} --- replace AGORA2 reference strains
  with personalised GEMs for tighter \(A_{ij}\) priors.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}

\textit{Same principle throughout: mechanistic structure makes scarce data yield testable models.}
\end{frame}

Interpretation: the crown C3D10 number (29.4 MPa) sits in the upper third of every
100-N/30° band consistent with a crown moment-arm geometry, and is at the lower edge
of the Cicciu 2018 150-N band after linear scaling, both as expected. The generic
(no-crown moment arm) sits in the lower third — the moment-arm ×1.5 ratio (crown /
generic) is then a within-cohort, dimensionally-controlled increment unaffected by
the absolute band uncertainty.
